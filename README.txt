Football 501 for your home screen

1. Put these files on any HTTPS host. GitHub Pages works: create a public repository, upload every file in this folder, then turn on Pages in Settings > Pages (deploy from the main branch, root folder).
2. Open the Pages link on your iPhone in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
3. Open it once with signal. After that it works with no connection, including on a plane.

Online rooms need internet on every phone. Solo and pass and play work offline.
When you upload a new version, open the app twice with signal to pick it up.
